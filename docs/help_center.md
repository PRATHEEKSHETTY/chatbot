# Northwind Cloud Storage - Help Center

## Plans and pricing
- Free: 5 GB of storage, $0.
- Plus: 200 GB of storage, $8 per month.
- Pro: 2 TB of storage, $20 per month.
- Team: 5 TB of pooled storage, $15 per user per month, minimum 3 users.
Annual billing saves 20% compared with paying monthly.

## File size limits
The maximum size of a single file is 2 GB on the Free plan and 50 GB on Plus, Pro and Team.

## Deleted files and version history
Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days on Free and Plus, and 90 days on Pro and Team. After that they are permanently deleted and support cannot recover them.
Version history is kept for 7 days on Free, 30 days on Plus, and 180 days on Pro and Team.

## Refunds
You can get a full refund within 14 days of your first purchase of a paid plan, whether monthly or annual. After 14 days we do not offer refunds, including prorated refunds. Request a refund in Settings > Billing > Request refund.

## Cancelling and downgrading
Cancel in Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Paid features stay active until the end of your current billing period. If a downgrade leaves you over the storage limit of the new plan, your account becomes read-only: you can download and delete files but not upload. Northwind keeps your files for 60 days after the downgrade so you can free up space or upgrade again.

## Support
Email support (support@northwind.example) is available on all plans and replies within 24 hours. Live chat is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC, on Pro and Team plans only. Phone support is not offered.

## Security and sign-in
Enable two-factor authentication in Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication using an authenticator app. SMS codes are not supported. When you enable it you receive 10 one-time recovery codes.
Password reset links expire after 1 hour.
Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Northwind does not offer end-to-end (zero-knowledge) encryption. Northwind is SOC 2 Type II certified. HIPAA workloads are not supported.

## Data regions
You choose the US or EU data region when you create your account. The region cannot be changed afterwards. No other regions are available.

## Sharing links
On Plus, Pro and Team you can set an expiry date and a password on share links. On Free, share links never expire and cannot be password protected.

## API
The API is available on Plus, Pro and Team, not on Free. The limit is 600 requests per minute per access token. Requests over the limit receive HTTP 429.

## Apps and platforms
Desktop app: Windows 10 or later, macOS 12 or later, Ubuntu 22.04 or later. Mobile apps: iOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later. Offline files are available on paid plans on desktop and mobile.
